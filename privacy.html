Privacy Policy

Application: Rclone. Operator: eosforge.

Last updated: September 11, 2026.

Purpose and data access

This integration is intended for the owner's personal use. Depending on the permissions granted, rclone can access Google Drive file contents and metadata, including names, sizes, timestamps and folder structure. Access is used to perform file listing, transfer and synchronization operations.

Storage and processing

OAuth access and refresh tokens are stored in the owner's rclone configuration. Local files, configuration, optional caches and logs remain on devices controlled by the owner. Files are transferred to or from the owner's Google Drive. When rclone crypt is used, encryption and decryption occur locally; local source and restored files may remain unencrypted.

Sharing and use

Google user data is not sold, used for advertising or used to train AI models. Transfers occur between the owner's devices and Google Drive as configured by the owner. The integration does not operate a separate data-collection server.

Retention and control

The owner controls retention of local and cloud files and can revoke OAuth access in their Google Account settings. Revoking access does not automatically delete existing files, local configuration, caches or logs.

This website

These pages do not contain analytics scripts or collect Google Drive credentials. GitHub Pages hosts the website and may process technical request information under GitHub's privacy statement.

Contact

Contact the operator. Do not post passwords, tokens, private files or configuration.
